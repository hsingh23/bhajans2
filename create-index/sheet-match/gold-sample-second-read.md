# Sheet-music links: independent second read of 100 random links by a different model

100 links drawn at random (seed 77) from the 1065 were read again by two Sonnet reviewers with the same brief as the Haiku full read. The dump for this pass showed the page where the automated scan scored best plus the next page, which for ten songs is one or two pages before the song's own page.

Result: 90 YES, 9 NO, 1 UNSURE. Every NO and the UNSURE was traced to that page choice: re-checked against the song's index page, each sheet's lyric 5-grams are found there (36 to 100 percent) and the first read had quoted the printed lines. Agreement between the two independent reads on the links themselves: 100 of 100; no wrong link found by either.
